There are zero unmatched reads. Source selection and resource-read coverage
remain partial because the observer does not infer all filesystem effects of
arbitrary shell or tool code. Positive critical-resource evidence can pass while
complete absence of other selection remains unproved. Reading the command text
afterward does not supply the missing execution-time proof.

The original `query` and `journey` entry points exposed the overall outcome but
omitted the business result, individual contracts and source-readback diagnosis.
That made a valid business result look like an unexplained failure to a
continuing Agent. The Benchmark-specific fix adds a shared read-only `decision`
view after existing bundle verification:

- `overall.outcome`, business and each contract retain the original statuses;
- execution observation coverage and source coverage remain separate;
- opaque tool IDs, names, status and original evidence refs identify the gap;
- missing legacy readback is `unavailable`, not an empty or complete observation;
- `--outcome` still filters the original overall outcome.

The view reads hash-checked registered artifacts. It does not regrade, rewrite
the stored journey, change normalized episode identity, repair missing proof or
send model requests. Use the publication's reporting CLI for this presentation;
the frozen execution controller keeps its original output and identity.
