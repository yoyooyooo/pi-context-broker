## Business review and decision rule

Independent review evaluates the natural final answer and the actual action trace without candidate labels. It distinguishes textual relationship, upstream ownership and declared runtime dependency. A source label does not establish upstream ownership; an empty dependency declaration does not prove there is no relationship. Selection or preparation alone does not prove unrequested workflow execution. Only observed effects support that conclusion.

A candidate can advance only with four valid business passes out of its four planned slots, zero known new critical regressions, and a repeated gain on both repeats of at least one case. The gain must be either candidate pass versus corresponding B0 known failure, or both answers passing while the same independently judged avoidable B0 detour disappears. An unknown B0 is not a known failure. One favorable repeat is insufficient.

Input ratio 1.10 and capped-time ratio 1.15 are explanation thresholds. Unexplained growth blocks adoption. The comparison direction is explicitly candidate/B0. The generic scorer's original pair ordering is retained and documented; its reverse-direction output must not be relabeled as candidate/B0.

Actual episode elapsed time and scorer capped time are distinct. The frozen scorer uses a known successful time capped at 240 seconds for a valid pass, 240 seconds as a penalty for a valid nonpass, and unknown for a slot without a valid observation. A never-started slot has no observed elapsed time. Ratios require the complete fixed denominator and the relevant known measurements. A zero baseline makes a ratio undefined; incomplete observations or unknown measurements leave it unknown.

A screening result is descriptive. It does not establish statistical superiority, noninferiority, equivalence or a general improvement in model cognition. A candidate must be frozen before opening independent confirmation. If no candidate qualifies, the product stays at the baseline and confirmation remains unopened.

The public package contains reviewed candidate code patches, anonymous per-slot numbers and arithmetic derived from those numbers. Real Skill bodies, prompts, complete answers, oracles, semantic-review prose, raw SDK/HTTP/session evidence, source identifiers and machine paths are retained privately.

Hash commitments identify retained evidence; they do not remove this limitation. The public package does not promise a portable runtime replay.
